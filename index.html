About My Mother

My mother, Ruth Atieno Sege, holds a special place in my heart. One of the things I admire most about her is the way she has continued taking care of us even after the loss of my father.

My father passed away when my parents had been married for only ten years. Despite this painful loss, my mother continued caring for us. Her dedication to our family is one of the reasons I respect and appreciate her so much.

To me, she represents the meaning of a good mother: someone whose love, care and commitment to her children deserve to be appreciated.

A Message to My Mother

Mum, thank you for continuing to take care of us even when life brought you a painful loss. I admire you for the mother you have been to us, and I am grateful for your love and dedication.

This tribute is a small way of showing my appreciation for you. You deserve to be celebrated not only for what you do, but also for the love and care you give as a mother.

With love and gratitude, always. ♥
